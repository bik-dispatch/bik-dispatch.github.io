Privacy policy

BMH Solutions LLC · effective October 16, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the "BMH Email Drafts" Google sign-in and the private tools that use it. They are used by one person, the owner of BMH Solutions LLC, on his own computer and his own Google account. They are not offered to anyone else.

What Google data the tools use

  • Read-only inbox access. For each new email: the sender's address, the subject, the date and Gmail's labels. For replies on the owner's own invoice emails, and for mail from brokers he has hauled for, the text of the email is read to look for a paperwork request or a payment hold.
  • Draft access. To create email drafts in the owner's Gmail with a load's invoice and paperwork attached.

How it is used

Only to alert the owner on his own phone when an email needs him (a payment, a paperwork request, a payment hold), and to prepare drafts he reviews and sends himself. The tools never send, delete, label, archive or mark email as read.

Where it is kept

Everything runs on the owner's computer. For each email the tools keep the sender's address, Gmail's message identifiers and their own short classification and reason. They do not store email subjects or email text; text read to look for a paperwork request is held in memory and discarded. Drafts live in the owner's own Gmail.

Sharing

None. Google user data is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or transferred to anyone else.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The locally kept records stay on the owner's computer under his control.
